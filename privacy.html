Lot Escape: Parking Puzzle — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026

Lot Escape is made by Neuron Studio. This policy explains what the game does with your information. In short: we don't collect any.

Information we collect

None. Lot Escape has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics, and no tracking. We don't collect, store, or share personal information, and we don't track you across other apps or websites.

Information stored on your device

Your progress (unlocked lots and stars), settings (sound and music), coins, and boosters are saved only on your device. They aren't sent to us. Deleting the app deletes this data.

In-app purchases

Coin packs are bought through Apple's App Store. Apple processes the payment under its own privacy policy; we never see your payment details. The game only receives a confirmation that a purchase succeeded so it can add your coins.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages and doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
